# RPGoal

A football career told with dice. Roll eight attributes, sign your first contract in your home country and play season by season on the d20, from the second division to the Champions League, the World Cup and the Ballon d'Or.

**Play:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/rpgoal/

## What's in here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole game: markup, styles and script in one file |
| `favicon.svg` | The d20 icon shown in the browser tab |

There is no build step and nothing to install. Open `index.html` in a browser to play locally.

## Saves

Careers and the Hall of Fame are stored in the browser's local storage, so they stay on the device and browser where they were played.

## Languages

English and Portuguese (Brazil), switchable in the top bar. Light and dark themes are included.
